
🌸 RealTalk – AI Postpartum Recovery Companion

📌 Overview

RealTalk is an AI-powered postpartum recovery platform designed to support mothers through their physical, emotional, and mental healing journey after childbirth. It provides personalized guidance, educational resources, AI-powered emotional support, and a safe community—all in one place.


---

✨ Features

🤖 AI Companion with Emotion Detection

📸 Private Recovery Photo Upload & Progress Tracking

🩺 Physical Care Guides (C-Section, Perineal Care, Breast Care, Hygiene, etc.)

❤️ Emotional Wellness Support

💕 Relationships & Intimacy Guidance

📚 Myth vs Fact Learning

📝 AI-Powered Daily Journal

👩‍👩‍👧 Mothers-Only Community

🍼 Mother & Baby Care Tips

🌸 Self-Care Library

🎓 Learning Center

👨‍👩‍👧 Partner Corner

🚨 Emergency Help & Warning Signs

📊 Personalized Recovery Dashboard

🚀 How It Works

1. Create an account and complete the onboarding process.


2. Access your personalized recovery dashboard.


3. Chat with the AI Companion for emotional support.


4. Explore physical recovery guides and educational resources.


5. Journal your daily thoughts and track your emotional wellbeing.


6. Upload private recovery photos to monitor healing over time.


7. Connect with other mothers through the community.


8. Receive reminders, affirmations, and personalized recommendations.




---

🎯 Objective

To empower postpartum mothers with reliable, AI-assisted guidance, emotional support, and evidence-based recovery resources in a safe and supportive environment.


---

🌍 Social Impact

RealTalk helps reduce postpartum isolation, promotes mental wellbeing, combats misinformation, and encourages healthy recovery by placing mothers' wellbeing at the center of postpartum care.


---

⚠️ Disclaimer

RealTalk is intended for educational and emotional support purposes only. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional healthcare advice. Users should consult qualified healthcare professionals for medical concerns or emergencies.
